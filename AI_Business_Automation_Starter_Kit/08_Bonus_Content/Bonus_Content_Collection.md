## Business Automation Roadmap

### Phase 1: Setup and discovery
- Identify your current lead sources and tools.
- Choose the first workflow based on your biggest time drain.
- Connect your CRM, email platform, and form builder.

### Phase 2: Launch core automations
- Implement lead capture and CRM entry.
- Automate welcome and confirmation messages.
- Set up appointment booking and reminder workflows.

### Phase 3: Optimize follow-up
- Add lead scoring and qualification triggers.
- Create nurture sequences for warm leads.
- Build a dashboard for pipeline visibility.

### Phase 4: Scale and refine
- Add additional automations for reviews, referrals, and repeat clients.
- Use AI prompts to power outreach and content.
- Monitor results and improve conversion at each stage.

### Phase 5: Ongoing growth
- Review metrics weekly.
- Adjust messaging based on performance.
- Expand automation to new campaigns and channels.

## AI Tools Cheat Sheet

### Recommended tools
- Zapier: connect apps and build automation triggers/actions.
- Make.com: build advanced scenario logic and multi-step workflows.
- ChatGPT: generate outreach, follow-up, and content.
- HubSpot/Pipedrive/Zoho: centralize leads, deals, and tasks.
- Google Sheets/Airtable: track leads, scores, and reports.
- Gmail/Outlook: send automated email communications.
- Calendly/Acuity: manage appointment bookings.
- Twilio: send SMS reminders.

### Best use cases
- Zapier: quick automation between two or three apps.
- Make.com: complex logic, iterators, and custom webhooks.
- ChatGPT: create messaging, rewrite copy, and brainstorm sequences.
- CRM: store lead data, follow-up tasks, and pipeline stages.
- Sheets/Airtable: keep lightweight tracking and dashboards.

### Quick tips
- Use templates for email subject lines and body copy.
- Always test automations with real data before going live.
- Monitor error logs daily for the first week.
- Use lead scoring to prioritize your best prospects.
- Keep personalization variables consistent across workflows.
