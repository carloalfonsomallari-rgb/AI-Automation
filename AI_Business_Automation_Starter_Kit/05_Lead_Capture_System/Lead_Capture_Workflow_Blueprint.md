# Lead Capture Workflow Blueprint

This blueprint maps the complete lead funnel from landing page to nurturing sequence, including scoring, landing page structure, and automation flow.

## Lead funnel map

1. Traffic source (Google Ads, Facebook, LinkedIn, referral)
2. Landing page visit
3. Lead capture form submission
4. CRM record creation
5. Lead score assignment
6. Automated welcome email
7. Lead nurturing sequence
8. Sales qualification and assignment
9. Proposal or appointment booking
10. Closed won / ongoing follow-up

## Landing page structure

### Section 1: Hero
- Headline: "Automate your appointments, follow-ups, and lead capture in hours."
- Subheadline: "AI Business Automation Starter Kit turns website visitors into qualified leads fast."
- Primary CTA: "Book a quick demo" or "Get instant access"
- Trust indicator: testimonial, client count, or small business focus

### Section 2: Problem statement
- Pain point: "Wasting hours on manual follow-up, scheduling, and lead tracking?"
- Short paragraph explaining the cost of missed leads and slow response.

### Section 3: Solution overview
- Explain the automation system:
  - Forms to CRM
  - Auto follow-up emails
  - Lead score and pipeline management
  - Appointment reminders

### Section 4: Lead capture form
- Fields:
  - Full name
  - Business email
  - Phone number
  - Company name
  - Primary challenge
  - Preferred contact method
- Microcopy: "We respond within 1 business hour."

### Section 5: Benefits
- Save 10+ hours weekly
- Close more leads without manual chasing
- Keep every opportunity visible
- Get follow-up reminders automatically

### Section 6: Social proof
- 3 customer quotes
- Use case bullets for coaches, gyms, agencies, and service businesses

### Section 7: Secondary CTA
- "Schedule a free automation review" or "Download the starter kit"

## Lead scoring system

Assign points based on actions and profile data:

- Form submission = 20 points
- Phone number provided = 10 points
- Service interest: high-value package = 15 points
- Requested a demo = 25 points
- Responded to welcome email = 10 points
- Opened first follow-up email = 5 points
- Clicked appointment link = 15 points
- Referred by existing customer = 20 points

### Scoring thresholds
- 0-30 = Cold lead
- 31-60 = Warm lead
- 61-100 = Hot lead
- 100+ = Priority follow-up

## Automation process diagram

1. Website form submission → 2. Lead enters CRM → 3. Lead score calculated → 4. Welcome email sent → 5. Lead category determines follow-up path

### Example process description
- When a new lead submits the landing page form, the system creates a CRM record, assigns an initial score, and sends an instant welcome email.
- If the lead score is high, they receive an accelerated nurture track with appointment scheduling. If the lead score is lower, they enter a value-building email sequence.

## Lead nurturing flow

### Stage 1: Immediate welcome
- Email 1: Thank you + next steps
- Purpose: confirm submission and share what to expect

### Stage 2: Value delivery
- Email 2: Pain point insight relevant to their industry
- Email 3: Case study or client success story
- Purpose: build trust and keep the lead engaged

### Stage 3: Qualification and call-to-action
- Email 4: Ask a qualification question or offer a discovery call
- Email 5: Send an appointment link with urgency
- Purpose: move the lead toward a sales conversation

### Stage 4: Follow-up and re-engagement
- Email 6: Reminder to book a call
- Email 7: Last chance message with value recap
- Purpose: recover interest and keep the lead warm

## Implementation notes

- Use form fields and hidden UTM values to record source, campaign, and offer.
- Keep the landing page simple and focused on one primary CTA.
- Customize the score weights for your service value and ideal customer profile.
- Monitor performance and adjust the nurturing flow based on open rates and conversion.
- Build a dashboard for lead score distribution and stage progression.
