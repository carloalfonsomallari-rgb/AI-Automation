# SOP Guides

This document contains standard operating procedures for launching and customizing the AI Business Automation Starter Kit.

## Quick start guide

### Purpose
Get your automation system live in under 2 hours.

### Steps
1. Open the appropriate folder for your business need.
2. Review the workflow that matches your current tools (Zapier or Make). 
3. Connect your apps: CRM, email, calendar, form builder.
4. Follow the setup instructions step-by-step.
5. Test each automation with a sample lead.
6. Confirm the data appears correctly in your CRM and email.
7. Activate the automation.

### Tips
- Use a dedicated business email for automations.
- Start with one workflow and scale gradually.
- Keep naming conventions consistent.

### Common mistakes
- Mapping fields incorrectly
- Using duplicate app accounts
- Skipping test data

## Workflow setup guide

### Purpose
Walk through the setup of your first automation.

### Steps
1. Choose the workflow that matches your goal.
2. Gather account access for the apps used in that workflow.
3. Authenticate each application in Zapier or Make.
4. Map fields carefully and review each step.
5. Test the full flow with a lead example.
6. Turn on the workflow.

### Screenshots placeholder
- Screenshot 1: App authorization
- Screenshot 2: Field mapping screen
- Screenshot 3: Zap/Mak scenario run log

### Tips
- Use a sample email address for test messages.
- Keep the CRM status field active.
- Save a copy of the workflow in your documentation.

### Common mistakes
- Not setting up filters
- Forgetting follow-up tasks
- Neglecting internal notifications

## Customization guide

### Purpose
Customize automations for your niche and workflow.

### Steps
1. Review the fields used in each workflow.
2. Update field names to match your CRM.
3. Add or remove steps based on your client journey.
4. Change email copy to reflect your brand voice.
5. Modify subject lines and CTAs for your audience.
6. Re-test the automation after changes.

### Screenshots placeholder
- Screenshot 1: Custom field selection
- Screenshot 2: Email template editing

### Tips
- Use consistent terminology across apps.
- Keep personalized variables like {{first_name}} and {{company_name}}.
- Review messaging for local spelling and tone.

### Common mistakes
- Over-customizing before testing
- Using too many conditional paths
- Forgetting to update follow-up timing

## Troubleshooting guide

### Purpose
Resolve common automation issues quickly.

### Steps
1. Check the Zapier/Make task history.
2. Review the error details or failed step.
3. Confirm app connections are active.
4. Verify field mapping is correct.
5. Re-run the workflow with test data.
6. Adjust filters or conditions if needed.

### Tips
- Use simple test cases.
- Keep a backup copy of the workflow.
- Turn off the automation before editing.

### Common mistakes
- Ignoring error messages
- Reusing stale test data
- Failing to check app permissions

## Beginner AI guide

### Purpose
Help non-technical users understand how AI fits into business automation.

### Steps
1. Read the workflow overview in this pack.
2. Identify the repetitive tasks you want to automate.
3. Choose one workflow and learn the app connections.
4. Use the ChatGPT prompt pack to generate outreach and follow-up messages.
5. Practice with one automation before expanding.

### Tips
- Start with a simple task such as email follow-up.
- Use the provided prompts as templates, not fixed scripts.
- Keep your messages conversational and relevant.

### Common mistakes
- Expecting AI to replace human judgment
- Copying prompts without personalization
- Skipping the review step before sending messages
